## The thesis
1. **Level 2 is a window, not a race.** Yunara + Leona hit 2 at 1:41, one second before the enemy, went straight in, and got a double kill at 1:59/2:02. Counter-proof: on Xayah we were 16 s *late* to 2, didn't force anything, and the lane still turned out fine.
2. **Kill → crash → back.** Once the enemy is dead, the back costs you nothing. Xayah: kill at 2:53, back at 3:16 (Caulfield's). Double at 4:17, back at 4:45 (Sheen). Yunara: double at 2:02, back at 2:26 (Hearthbound Axe). See [[Wave crash]] and [[Recall timing]].
3. **A lead turns their gank into your gift.** Yunara at 4:08-4:16: Cho'Gath (their jungler) ganks a lane that is 32 CS to 13 up. We win the 2v3 3-for-0. See [[Outnumbered fight]].
4. **Quiet lane? Farm it, back early, move first, and respect the fog.** Kai'sa won level 2 against Ezreal + Braum with nothing to all-in. Instead: an early back at 2:24 (Cull), 43 CS to 32 at 5:00, then 3 kills in the 4:16-4:34 fight. The price came at 6:11: caught alone by Rek'Sai + Ezreal + Braum.

Proof stat for the intro (Riot timeline): **first 5 minutes, all three games together: 10 kills, 0 deaths** (Xayah 3, Kai'sa 3, Yunara 4).
