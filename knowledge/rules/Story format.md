# Story format
The owner-approved base for every game: the early game, the first back, every
fight that moved the score, and the close-out. A video covers 2-3 games
([[Story-join]]). Owner asks ("cinematic last teamfight", "show me walking out
of base at 20:05") are added on top as requests; they don't replace the plan.
