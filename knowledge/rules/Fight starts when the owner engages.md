# Fight starts when the owner engages
A fight chapter starts when the owner engages ([[Approach, engage, outcome]]),
not while a teammate fights off to the side. It still starts before the first
damage, never at the kill.

> Owner: "the first 5 seconds seems redundant as i'm not fighting. that's my teammate"
