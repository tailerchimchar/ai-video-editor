# Noodlz content knowledge

The one place for what the owner has taught: League knowledge, editing rules,
content series and games. Open this folder in Obsidian ("Open folder as
vault"); the graph view shows how everything links.

- **[[Concepts]]**: League game knowledge, in the owner's words.
- **[[Rules]]**: how a video is edited. Every rule links the video that taught it.
- **[[Series]]**: [[Solo queue]] and [[Team games (ACL)]].
- **[[Games]]**: one short note per recorded game.

## How it is maintained
- When the owner teaches something in chat, Claude adds or updates a note
  here and links it (owner decision 2026-11-26).
- The owner can edit any note in Obsidian, and the edited text is what counts.
- Superseded rules are kept and marked `status: superseded` with a link to
  the replacement, so the history stays readable.
- Notes stay short. Times are game clock (what the HUD shows) unless a note
  says "recording seconds".
- Opponents are named by champion only. No teammate quotes, no mic transcripts.
