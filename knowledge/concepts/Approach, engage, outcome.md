# Approach, engage, outcome
The owner's boundaries of a play:
- **Approach:** the two sides enter each other's range (for an ADC, about
  auto-attack range). Aimless lane play before that is not the approach. A
  shotcall can start it.
- **Engage:** the first committed fighting.
- **Outcome:** the play is over: the owner is safe or the chase stops, an
  objective falls, or the team calls the next move.

Plays are rated for impressiveness (out of 4) and game relevance (out of 10).
Low impressiveness with high relevance is story material, not montage. A
level-1 trade with no kill still counts as a play.
