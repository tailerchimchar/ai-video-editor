# viewer-expert dictionary

Format per entry: **term** · plain definition · how to spot it in a clip.

- **hook** · the first 1-3 seconds; the reason not to swipe · payoff visible, or a promise (title card + motion) that the payoff is seconds away.
- **swipe point** · a moment a viewer leaves · dead air > 2s, scoreboard, slow walk to lane, unreadable caption.
- **payoff** · the moment the viewer came for · killfeed pop, enemy turning, a line that lands.
- **pattern interrupt** · a cut, zoom or SFX that resets attention · use before a swipe point, not after.
- **loop** · last frame flows into first · earns replays, which platforms reward.
- **retention curve** · platform graph of viewers vs time · paste into log.md as `analytics:`; the first dip is the real swipe point.
