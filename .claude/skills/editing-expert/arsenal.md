# Arsenal: what this pipeline can render today

GENERATED by registry.py. Do not edit by hand: change the registry, then run
`python -m ai_video_editor.registry --write`.

## Formats (edit plan `format`)
`hype_montage` · `story_short` · `vo_short` · `funny_short` · `arc_montage` (planned, not renderable yet)

## Per-clip and per-video effects (edit plan `effects[].kind`)

| kind | params | scope | MCP tool | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `zoom` | `factor`, `roi` | clip | `zoom_clip`, `zoom_compilation_clip` | Zoom on an ROI preset (center, scoreline_lol, minimap_lol, full). |
| `focus` | `x`, `y`, `radius`, `dim` | clip | `focus_clip`, `focus_compilation_clip` | Dim everything outside a circle at (x, y) fractions of the frame. |
| `caption` | `text`, `aspect` | clip | `caption_clip`, `caption_compilation_clip`, `tiktok_caption_clip`, `tiktokify_clip_captions`, `segment_caption_clip`, `edit_clip_captions`, `add_clip_caption`, `remove_clip_caption` | Caption a whole clip; omit text to use the transcript. Classic style via `caption_clip`, tiktok style via `tiktok_caption_clip`. |
| `caption_video` | `segments`, `aspect` | short | `caption_video` | Burn timed caption segments onto a whole video by path. |
| `beat_flash` | `beat_flash`, `beat_flash_source` | short | `compile_shorts` | Flash on detected beats or kills; also a flag on the montage tools. |

Not renderable yet (reject or substitute): `slow_mo`, `speed_ramp`, `transition`, `pulse_zoom`, `beat_snap`, `time_scoped`.

## Sound (edit plan `sfx[].sound`, `music`)

| kind | params | scope | MCP tool | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sfx` | `sfx` | short | `add_sfx_at` | One-shot sound effects at timestamps; ducks the source audio. |
| `music` | `music_path` | short | `compile_shorts` | Music bed under a short or montage (flat mix, no envelope). |

| op | MCP tool |
|---|---|
| extract an SFX from footage into the library | `extract_sfx` |
| download / cache a song (YouTube URL or local file) | `download_music` |

SFX names come from `WORKSPACE/media_library/<game>/sfx/`. Ask the session
to list the folder before naming one.
